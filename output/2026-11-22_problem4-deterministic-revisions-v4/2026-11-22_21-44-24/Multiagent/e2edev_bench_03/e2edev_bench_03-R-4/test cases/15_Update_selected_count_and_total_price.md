# Update selected count and total price

Case ID · G-015

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-4

Verify that the count of selected seats displayed with data-testid 'selected-count' updates to reflect the current number of seats with the 'selected' class, and the total price displayed with data-testid 'total-price' is calculated as the product of the number of selected seats and the current ticket price.
