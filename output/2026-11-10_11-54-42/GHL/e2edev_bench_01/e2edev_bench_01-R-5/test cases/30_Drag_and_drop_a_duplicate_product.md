# Drag and drop a duplicate product

Case ID · G-030

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Condition: product element is draggable with valid title and price; drop area exists; product already present in cart; drop performed. Action: system initiates drag event capturing title and price (R-1), increments quantity in .box1 while keeping title and unit price unchanged (R-3), updates total price accordingly (R-5).
