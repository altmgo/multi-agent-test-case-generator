# Random Selection Process on Enter Key Press

Case ID · G-003

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_66-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-3

Verify that pressing the 'Enter' key clears the textarea and initiates a random selection highlighting process for 3 seconds across 30 iterations, culminating in one finally highlighted tag.
