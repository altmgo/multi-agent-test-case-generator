# e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1

When the user drags a product item (li element) from the product list, the system should initiate a drag event, capturing the product's title and price for transfer. The product items are represented as 'li' elements with the attribute 'draggable' set to 'true'. Each product item contains an image, a title, and a price, identified by data-testid attributes such as 'product-item-1', 'product-title-1', and 'product-price-1'. The drag event should capture the title and price of the product being dragged.
