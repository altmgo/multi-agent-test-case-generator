# Form Submission with Missing Required Fields

Case ID · G-020

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_30-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_30-R-2

Attempt to submit the badge form by clicking 'generate-badge-button' while leaving one or more required fields ('eventname', 'name', 'designation', 'company', 'access') empty. Verify that the form container remains visible and the badge container is not displayed.
