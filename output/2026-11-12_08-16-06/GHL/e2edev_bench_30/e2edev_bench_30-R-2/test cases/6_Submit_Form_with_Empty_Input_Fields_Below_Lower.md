# Submit Form with Empty Input Fields \(Below Lower Limit\)

Case ID · G-006

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_30-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_30-R-2

Test form submission when one or more required fields ('eventname', 'name', 'designation', 'company') are left empty (0 characters, below the minimum required boundary). Verify that the form remains visible and the badge/download button are not displayed.
