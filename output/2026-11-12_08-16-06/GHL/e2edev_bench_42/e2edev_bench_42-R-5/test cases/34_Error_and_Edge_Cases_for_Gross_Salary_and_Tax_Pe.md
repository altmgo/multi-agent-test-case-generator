# Error and Edge Cases for Gross Salary and Tax Percentage

Case ID · G-034

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_42-R-5, e2edev\_bench\_42-R-6, e2edev\_bench\_42-R-9

Verify that inputting negative or zero gross salary handles calculations gracefully by defaulting to $0.00 for net earnings, and that zero or negative tax percentages result in '-$0.00' tax deductions without affecting other calculations.
