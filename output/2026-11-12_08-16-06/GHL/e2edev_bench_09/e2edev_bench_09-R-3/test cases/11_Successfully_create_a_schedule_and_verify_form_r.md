# Successfully create a schedule and verify form reset

Case ID · G-011

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_09-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_09-R-3

User navigates to the 'Study Planner' page, fills out the 'Subject', 'Date', and 'Time' fields in the schedule form, and clicks the 'Add Schedule' button. The system validates the fields, appends the new schedule item to the schedule list in the correct format, and resets the form input fields to empty.
