# Display weekday and quote for Saturday

Case ID · G-003

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_46-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_46-R-2

Verify that on a local date representing Saturday (upper boundary of days of the week), the application automatically displays Saturday in the span with data-testid='weekday' and the corresponding quote in the span with data-testid='phrase' upon page load.
