# Forbidden Transition: Clicking Prev Button on Step 1 \(Edge Case\)

Case ID · G-015

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_52-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_52-R-4

Verify that rapid or extra clicks on the 'Prev' button when already at Step 1 are handled gracefully, ensuring the first circle remains active, the progress bar width does not fall below 0%, and the 'Prev' button remains disabled.
