# Transfer valid text successfully

Case ID · G-001

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_37-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-4, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-6

Verify that clicking the move-text-button when the textarea contains valid text (within 280 characters, including special characters) successfully moves the text to the content-display area, clears the textarea, updates the progress indicator and progress bar items sequentially, and temporarily changes the button background to #ccc.
