# Successful text transfer with progress update and clearing textarea

Case ID · G-014

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_37-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-4, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-6

User enters text into the textarea, clicks the move text button, and verifies that the text is transferred character by character to the content display area, the textarea is cleared, the button background changes to '#ccc' during transfer and reverts, and the progress indicator and progress bar items update correctly.
