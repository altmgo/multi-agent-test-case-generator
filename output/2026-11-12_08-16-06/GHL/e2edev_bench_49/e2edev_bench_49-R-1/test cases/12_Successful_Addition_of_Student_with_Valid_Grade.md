# Successful Addition of Student with Valid Grade

Case ID · G-012

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_49-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_49-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_49-R-5

Navigate to the Student Grade Book interface, enter a valid student name in 'student-name', enter a valid grade within 0-100 in 'student-grade', and click 'add-student-button'. Verify that the student is added to 'student-list' in the format 'Name: Grade' with a unique data-id attribute ('student-item-1') and the form fields are reset to empty.
