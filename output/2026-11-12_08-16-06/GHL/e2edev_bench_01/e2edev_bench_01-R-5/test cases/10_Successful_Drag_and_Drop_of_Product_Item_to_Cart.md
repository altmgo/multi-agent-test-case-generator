# Successful Drag and Drop of Product Item to Cart

Case ID · G-010

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

User initiates a drag event on a draggable product item capturing its title and price, then drops it into the designated drop area to add it to the cart, verifying that the cart display updates and the total price is calculated correctly.
