# Calculate HCF with valid inputs and check formatting and parity indicators

Case ID · G-008

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_63-R-6, e2edev\_bench\_63-R-8

Verify that clicking the HCF submit button computes the HCF for valid positive integers, displays the result formatted as 'HCF of [num1] and [num2] is [result]' in 'hcf-result-display', and correctly adjusts the opacity of 'hcf-even-display' and 'hcf-odd-display' based on parity.
