# Invalid Principal Input \(Zero or Negative\)

Case ID · G-025

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_24-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_24-R-5, e2edev\_bench\_24-R-6

Enter a value less than or equal to 0 in the 'Principal' field while other fields have valid inputs. Click 'Calculate' or press 'Enter'. Verify that an alert with the message 'Please Enter Valid Values!!' is displayed and calculation is prevented.
