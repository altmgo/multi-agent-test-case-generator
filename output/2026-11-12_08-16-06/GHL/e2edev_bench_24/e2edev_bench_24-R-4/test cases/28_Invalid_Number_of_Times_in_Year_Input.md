# Invalid Number of Times in Year Input

Case ID · G-028

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_24-R-4, e2edev\_bench\_24-R-5, e2edev\_bench\_24-R-6

Enter a zero, negative, or non-numeric value in the 'Number of Times in a Single Year' field while other fields are valid. Trigger calculation. Verify that the alert 'Please Enter Valid Values!!' appears and no calculation occurs.
