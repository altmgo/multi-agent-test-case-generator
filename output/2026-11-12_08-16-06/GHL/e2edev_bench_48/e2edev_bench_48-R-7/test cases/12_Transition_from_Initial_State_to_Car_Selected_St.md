# Transition from Initial State to Car Selected State

Case ID · G-012

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_48-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_48-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_48-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_48-R-7

Verify that clicking the 'Choose Sedan', 'Choose SUV', or 'Choose Convertible' button transitions the system from the Initial State to the Car Selected state, updates the 'Car Type' field, and clears the 'Total Price' field.
