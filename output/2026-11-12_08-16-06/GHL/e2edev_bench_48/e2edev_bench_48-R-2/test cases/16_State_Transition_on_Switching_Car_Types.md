# State Transition on Switching Car Types

Case ID · G-016

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_48-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_48-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_48-R-3

Verify that switching between different car types (e.g., Sedan to SUV or Convertible) resets the 'Total Price' and correctly updates the 'Car Type' field while maintaining a valid state transition.
