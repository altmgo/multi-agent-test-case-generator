# Normal Workflow - Enter choices and trigger random selection

Case ID · G-011

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_66-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-3

Verify that upon loading, the textarea automatically receives focus. The user types comma-separated choices, which are dynamically displayed as tags with unique data-id attributes. Upon pressing the 'Enter' key, the textarea is cleared, a 3-second highlighting sequence (30 iterations at 100ms) runs, and one final tag remains highlighted.
