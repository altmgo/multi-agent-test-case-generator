# Reverse Selection Inversion with Mixed Initial States

Case ID · G-027

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_57-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_57-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_57-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_57-R-4

Error guessing test to ensure that clicking the 'Reverse Selection' link ('checkReverse') accurately inverts the state of every individual checkbox (from 'checkbox-1' to 'checkbox-10') when starting from a mixed checked/unchecked state, and correctly updates the 'Select All/None' control afterwards.
