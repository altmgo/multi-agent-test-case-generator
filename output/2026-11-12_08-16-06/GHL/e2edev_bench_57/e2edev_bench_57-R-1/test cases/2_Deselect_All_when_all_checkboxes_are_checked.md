# Deselect All when all checkboxes are checked

Case ID · G-002

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_57-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_57-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_57-R-4

Verify that clicking the 'Select All/None' checkbox ('checkAll') when all individual checkboxes ('checkbox-1' to 'checkbox-10') are checked deselects all individual checkboxes, unchecks 'checkAll', and updates 'checkAll-label' to '全选'.
