# Distinguish seat statuses via legends and interact with available, occupied, and selectable seats

Case ID · G-014

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-5

Verify that users can distinguish between available, selected, and occupied seats through legends (such as data-testid = 'seat-select'), that occupied seats are non-interactive and prevent selection, that available seats can be clicked to toggle their selection, and that selecting or deselecting seats updates the count of selected seats and the total price accordingly.
