# Select available seat and update count and total price

Case ID · G-010

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-4

Click on available seats without the 'occupied' class to toggle the 'selected' class, and verify that the count of selected seats in data-testid 'selected-count' and the total price in data-testid 'total-price' update correctly as the product of the number of selected seats and the current ticket price.
