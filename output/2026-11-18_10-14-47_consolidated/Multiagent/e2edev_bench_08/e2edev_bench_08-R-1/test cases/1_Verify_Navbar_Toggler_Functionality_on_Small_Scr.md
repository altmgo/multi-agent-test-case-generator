# Verify Navbar Toggler Functionality on Small Screens

Case ID · G-001

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_08-R-1

Load the page on a small screen where the navigation menu is initially collapsed. Click the navbar toggler button (data-testid="navbar-toggler") and verify that the navigation menu (data-testid="navbarSupportedContent") expands to show menu items. Click the toggler button again and verify that the menu collapses.
