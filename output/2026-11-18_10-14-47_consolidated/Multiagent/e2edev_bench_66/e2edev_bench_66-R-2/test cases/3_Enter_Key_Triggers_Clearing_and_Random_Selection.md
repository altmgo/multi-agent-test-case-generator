# Enter Key Triggers Clearing and Random Selection Animation

Case ID · G-003

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_66-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-2, e2edev\_bench\_66-R-3

Verify that pressing the 'Enter' key clears the textarea and initiates a 3-second random selection process (30 iterations at 100ms intervals) that toggles the 'highlight' class on tags, culminating in one final highlighted tag.
