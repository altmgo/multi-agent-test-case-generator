Case ID · G-001

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_37-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_37-R-3

Verify that inputting text into 'text-area' and clicking 'move-text-button' moves the text to 'content-display', clears the textarea, updates the 'progress-indicator', changes the button background to '#ccc' during the transfer, and reverts it upon completion.
