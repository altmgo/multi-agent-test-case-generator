# Drop Product into Drop Area and Update Cart Display

Case ID · G-002

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3

Verify that dropping a product item into the designated drop area (id='div1', data-testid='drop-area') successfully adds it to the cart and updates the cart display (.box1 for quantity, .box2 for title, and .box3 for unit price), supporting both new and repeated products.
