Case ID · G-004

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_03-R-4

Precondition: Seats are already selected and a movie is chosen. Steps: Change the movie selection from the dropdown (data-testid="movie-select"). Expected: The total price updates according to the new ticket price while maintaining the existing count of selected seats.
