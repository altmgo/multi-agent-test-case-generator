# Initiate drag event on product item

Case ID · G-001

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1

Precondition: The product list is rendered with draggable product items containing data-testid attributes such as 'product-item-1', 'product-title-1', and 'product-price-1'. Steps: Drag the product item 'product-item-1'. Expected Behavior: The system initiates a drag event capturing the product's title and price for transfer.
