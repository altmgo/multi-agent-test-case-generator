# Calculate and display total price for multiple and repeated products

Case ID · G-005

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Precondition: Multiple products, including repeated items, have been dropped into the drop area id='div1' (data-testid='drop-area'). Steps: System calculates the total price by summing the prices of all individual and repeated products in the cart. Expected Behavior: The total price is formatted to two decimal places with the appropriate currency symbol (e.g., '$80.00') and displayed in the <div id='allMoney'> element.
