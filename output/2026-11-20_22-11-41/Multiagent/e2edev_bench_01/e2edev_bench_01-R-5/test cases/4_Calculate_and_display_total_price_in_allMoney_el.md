# Calculate and display total price in allMoney element

Case ID · G-004

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Precondition: Products have been added to the cart. Steps: System calculates the total price by summing the prices of all products in the cart. Expected Behavior: The total is formatted to two decimal places with the appropriate currency symbol (e.g., '$80.00') and displayed in the <div id='allMoney'> element.
