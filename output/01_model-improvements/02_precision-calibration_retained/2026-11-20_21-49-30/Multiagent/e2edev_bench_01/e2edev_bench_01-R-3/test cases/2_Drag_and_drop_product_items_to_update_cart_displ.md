# Drag and drop product items to update cart display

Case ID · G-002

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3

Verify that dragging and dropping a product item into the designated drop area (id='div1', data-testid='drop-area') updates the cart display such that the product title appears in .box2, the unit price in .box3, and the quantity in .box1, supporting both different and repeated products.
