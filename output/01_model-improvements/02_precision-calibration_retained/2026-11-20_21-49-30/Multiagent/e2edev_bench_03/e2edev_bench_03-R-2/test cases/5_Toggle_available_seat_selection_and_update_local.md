# Toggle available seat selection and update localStorage and UI

Case ID · G-005

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-2

Verify that clicking on an available seat (e.g., identified by data-testid="seat-1-1") toggles its selection status, visually marks it, stores its indices in localStorage, and updates the seat count (data-testid="selected-count") and total price (data-testid="total-price").
