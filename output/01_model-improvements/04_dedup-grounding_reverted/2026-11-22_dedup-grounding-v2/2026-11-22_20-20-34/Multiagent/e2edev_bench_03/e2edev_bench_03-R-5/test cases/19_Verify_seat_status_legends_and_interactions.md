# Verify seat status legends and interactions

Case ID · G-019

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-5

Verify that the page provides legends to distinguish between available, selected, and occupied seats using corresponding identifiers such as data-testid = 'seat-select', occupied seats are non-interactive to prevent selection, available seats can be clicked to toggle selection, and seat counts and total prices update accordingly when seats are selected or deselected.
