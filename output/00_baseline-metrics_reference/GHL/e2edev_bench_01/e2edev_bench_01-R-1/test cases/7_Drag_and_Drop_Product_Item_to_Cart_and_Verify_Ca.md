# Drag and Drop Product Item to Cart and Verify Cart Display and Total Price

Case ID · G-007

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

User drags a product item from the product list and drops it into the designated drop area. The system initiates the drag event capturing title and price, updates the cart display showing quantity, title, and unit price, and calculates the total price displayed in the allMoney element.
