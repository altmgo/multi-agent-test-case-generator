# Successful Drag and Drop of a Single Product to Cart

Case ID · G-038

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Verify that a user can successfully drag a product item (e.g., data-testid='product-item-1') and drop it into the designated drop area (id='div1', data-testid='drop-area'), and the cart display automatically updates with the product title in .box2, unit price in .box3, and quantity in .box1.
