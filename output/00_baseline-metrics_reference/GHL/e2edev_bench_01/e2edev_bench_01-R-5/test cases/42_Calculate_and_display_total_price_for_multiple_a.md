Case ID · G-042

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Drag multiple distinct and repeated product items into the drop area, and verify that the system correctly sums all product prices, formats the total to two decimal places with the currency symbol, and displays it in the id='allMoney' element according to requirement e2edev_bench_01-R-5.
