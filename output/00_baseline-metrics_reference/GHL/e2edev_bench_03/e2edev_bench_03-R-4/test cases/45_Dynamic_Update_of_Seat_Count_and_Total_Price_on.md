# Dynamic Update of Seat Count and Total Price on Seat Selection and Movie Choice

Case ID · G-045

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-4

Verify that when a user selects a movie from the dropdown menu (data-testid 'movie-select') and clicks available seats, the selected seat count (data-testid 'selected-count') updates and the total price (data-testid 'total-price') calculates correctly as the product of the number of selected seats and the ticket price.
