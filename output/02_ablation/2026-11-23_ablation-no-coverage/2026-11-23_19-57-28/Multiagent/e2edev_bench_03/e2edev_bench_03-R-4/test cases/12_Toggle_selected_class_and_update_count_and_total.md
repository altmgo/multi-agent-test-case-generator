# Toggle selected class and update count and total price on available seat click

Case ID · G-012

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_03-R-4

Verify that clicking on available seats (those without the 'occupied' class) toggles the 'selected' class on the clicked seat, updates the selected seat count (data-testid 'selected-count'), and calculates the total price (data-testid 'total-price') as the product of the number of selected seats and the current ticket price.
