# Drop product item into drop area to add to cart and update display

Case ID · G-002

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3

Verify that dragging and dropping a product item (e.g., data-testid='product-item-1') into the designated drop area (id='div1', data-testid='drop-area') adds it to the cart and automatically updates the cart display with the product title in .box2, the unit price in .box3, and the quantity in .box1.
