# Drop product into drop area to add to cart

Case ID · G-002

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3

Verify that dragging and dropping a product item (e.g., data-testid="product-item-1") into the designated drop area (id="div1", data-testid="drop-area") updates the cart display automatically: the product title appears in .box2, the unit price in .box3, and the quantity in .box1.
