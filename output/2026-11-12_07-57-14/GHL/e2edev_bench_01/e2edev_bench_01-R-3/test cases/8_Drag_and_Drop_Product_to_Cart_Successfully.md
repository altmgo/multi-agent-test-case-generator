# Drag and Drop Product to Cart Successfully

Case ID · G-008

Requirements · e2edev\_bench\_01-R-1, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-3, e2edev\_bench\_01-R-5

Verify that a user can drag a product item from the product list and drop it into the designated drop area to add it to the cart. Ensure that the cart display automatically updates the product title in .box2, the unit price in .box3, the quantity in .box1, and calculates the total price in the #allMoney element.
